## 2. Two roles: controller and processor

2.1 **nonagentic.ai as processor.** The Service is used by organisations ("**Customers**"). For customer content — datasets, flows, comments, votes, prompts to and responses from the builder assistant, and the accounts of members the Customer invites — the Customer is the controller and nonagentic.ai processes the data on its behalf as processor (Article 28 GDPR). For that processing, **the privacy notice of your organisation applies**, and you should direct requests to your organisation. nonagentic.ai will forward requests it receives to the relevant Customer.

2.2 **nonagentic.ai as controller.** nonagentic.ai is controller for its own purposes: account administration of each Customer's admin contact, security logs, handling of signup requests and invitations, and service improvement using aggregated data only. This notice covers that processing.

## 6. Security

6.1 Measures include TLS for all traffic, encrypted database connections, tenant isolation in the database, role-based access, automated masking of personal data for non-privileged users, sandboxed test runs, encrypted storage of customer API keys, an audit log, monitoring, nightly backups with an automatic restore test, and restricted administrative access. The Data Processing Agreement (Annex II) lists the measures in full.

## 7. Your rights

7.1 Under the GDPR you have the right to:
a. access your personal data (Art. 15);
b. rectification of inaccurate data (Art. 16);
c. erasure (Art. 17);
d. restriction of processing (Art. 18);
e. data portability (Art. 20);
f. object to processing based on legitimate interests (Art. 21);
g. withdraw consent at any time, where processing is based on consent, without affecting earlier processing (Art. 7(3)). We do not currently rely on consent for the purposes in clause 3.

7.2 Send requests to george@nonagentic.ai, or, for data your organisation controls, to your organisation's administrator. We respond within one month (Art. 12(3)), extendable by two months for complex requests. We may need to verify your identity.

7.3 You have the right to lodge a complaint with the Dutch supervisory authority, the Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens (https://autoriteitpersoonsgegevens.nl), or with the authority in your EU country of residence or work.

## 8. Is providing data required?

8.1 An email address and name are required to request access or to have an account; without them we cannot admit you. Network data such as IP addresses is necessarily processed to deliver the Service securely. Other data, such as the note on a signup request, is optional.

## 9. Automated decision-making

9.1 We do not make decisions based solely on automated processing that produce legal effects or similarly significantly affect you (Art. 22 GDPR). The weekly or periodic voting on solutions is done by human members, not by an automated system. The builder assistant is an AI system you interact with (Article 50 AI Act); its output is a suggestion for you to review, not a decision about you.

## 10. Cookies and local storage

10.1 When you log in, the Service sets the strictly necessary session cookies of its identity service (Keycloak), which are required to log you in and keep you logged in.

10.2 The Service uses your browser's local storage only for preferences you set: language, sidebar state and the active organisation.

10.3 The public signup form uses Cloudflare Turnstile, a bot-protection check that may store or read technical information on your device only to verify that the request is not automated. This is strictly necessary for the security of a service you request, is exempt from consent on the same basis as described in clause 10.5, and is not used for tracking or advertising.

10.4 We do not use analytics, advertising or tracking cookies or similar technologies.

10.5 Storing or accessing information on your device that is strictly necessary to provide a service you explicitly request is exempt from consent under Article 5(3) of the ePrivacy Directive 2002/58/EC, implemented in the Netherlands in Article 11.7a of the Telecommunications Act (*Telecommunicatiewet*). For that reason the Service does not show a cookie consent banner.
