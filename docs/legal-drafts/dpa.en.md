## Annex I — Description of the processing

**A. Parties.** Controller: the Customer (contact: its organisation administrator). Processor: nonagentic.ai, george@nonagentic.ai.

**B. Subject matter.** Provision of CommonPractice: organisations post improvement challenges; members build and test small automation flows from building blocks, test them on sample data, and vote on solutions on a configurable cycle.

**C. Duration.** The term of the Terms plus the export and deletion periods in clause 10.

**D. Nature of processing.** Hosting, storage, structuring, retrieval, display, masking, executing test runs in sandboxes, generating AI-assisted responses, transmission (including to a Customer-configured AI provider), backup, and deletion.

**E. Purpose.** Providing the Service to the Customer and its Users, including security and support of that provision.

**F. Categories of data subjects.**
1. Users invited by the Customer (employees, contractors or other members).
2. Data subjects of the Customer whose personal data appear in datasets the Customer uploads for testing (for example the Customer's clients, employees or suppliers).
3. Persons mentioned in comments, challenges, flows or builder-chat content.

**G. Types of personal data.**
1. Member account data: email, name, login identifier, locale, role, organisation.
2. User content: comments, votes, builder-chat prompts and responses (stored), flows.
3. Audit log: actor, action, metadata.
4. Usage metering: token counts and cost per User (not prompt text).
5. Customer datasets: any personal data the Customer chooses to upload, which may include names, email addresses, telephone numbers, IBANs and BSNs. Users without the data-manager role see masked values; these five types are automatically detected and masked. Data managers control which columns are shown unmasked.
6. Technical data: IP address and client host in logs.

**H. Special categories.** None are intended. The Customer may upload special categories (Art. 9 GDPR) as sample data only where it has a legal basis and has configured masking for the relevant columns.

**I. Retention.** Customer content and personal data: for the term, then as in clause 10. Application logs: 24 hours. Metrics: 2 days. Database logs (client host): about 10 weeks. Backups: 14 days.

**J. Processing locations.** Germany (application, database and identity service at STRATO / IONOS SE, Berlin and Frankfurt); the Netherlands (language model and nightly backups on nonagentic.ai's own hardware); sub-processors as in Annex III.

## Annex II — Technical and organisational measures (Art. 32)

1. **Encryption in transit.** TLS for all traffic to the Service; mutual TLS between internal services. Database connections are encrypted. Nightly backups are transferred encrypted in transit.
2. **Authentication.** Database passwords are stored using scram-sha-256. Customer API keys for AI providers are stored encrypted with AES-256-GCM.
3. **Tenant isolation.** Strict separation between organisations, enforced in the database by row-level security.
4. **Access control.** Role-based access within the Service (member, developer, data manager, organisation admin).
5. **Data minimisation for Users.** Automated detection and masking of personal data (names, email addresses, telephone numbers, IBANs, BSNs) for Users without the data-manager role; data managers control which columns are unmasked.
6. **Sandboxing.** Test runs of user flows are executed in isolated lightweight virtual machines.
7. **Logging.** Audit log of administrative actions, with a daily export.
8. **Monitoring.** Monitoring and alerting of the Service and infrastructure.
9. **Availability and resilience.** Nightly backups, kept 14 days, with an automatic restore test.
10. **Administrative access.** Access to production is limited to named administrators over a private network with key-based authentication.
11. **Physical security.** Provided by the hosting provider's certified data centres.
12. **Planned.** Storage encryption at rest is planned. It is not yet in place for the primary database.
